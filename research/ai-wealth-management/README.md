# AI Wealth Management Research

Research and analysis on AI-powered wealth management, behavioral coaching, and automated investment advisory systems.

## Reports

- [Behavioral Coaching in AI Wealth Management](./behavioral-coaching-ai-wealth-management-report.md) — Comprehensive analysis of behavioral finance biases, AI detection/intervention architecture, and product design for an AI wealth advisor.

## Key Topics

- **Behavioral Coaching**: Detecting and correcting investor biases (loss aversion, herding, recency bias, overconfidence) in real-time
- **Auto-Research Systems**: Building closed-loop research systems with robust feedback loops for market intelligence
- **AI Wealth Advisor**: Architecture for decision-support vs. decision-making in financial advisory
- **Regulatory Framework**: SEC/FINRA considerations for AI financial advice

## Goals

- Evaluate market opportunity for AI wealth advisory
- Design MVP behavioral coaching system
- Explore integration with Zoro's wealth platform
