# Research Dossier: India's Cognitive Capacity Bottleneck

## Core Concept

The arXiv paper by Kwan Soo Shin (August 2026) introduces the Effective Cognitive Population (ECP) framework as a metric for national planning in the AI era. [3]

**Formula:** ECP = N × H × (1 + AC)
- N = population size
- H = Human Capital Index Plus (HCI+) from the World Bank, representing cognitive capacity
- A = AI Opportunity Index (digital infrastructure + innovation integration from IMF)
- C = Conversion governance factor (regulation + ethics from IMF)

**Key findings:**
- Tested across 144 countries
- ECP raises R-squared from 0.849 (HCI+-adjusted stock) to 0.882 for predicting 2024 output
- Leave-one-country-out RMSE drops from 0.723 to 0.641
- 89 of 144 countries move at least 10 rank positions from headcount, mostly through the human capital adjustment
- The A×C interaction is not statistically supported; it serves as a planning rule rather than causal complementarity
- ECP is diagnostic, not a forecast tool

## India-Specific Data Points

### Education Quality
- 1.5 million engineers graduate annually from Indian institutions [1]
- Only 20% of Indian engineers are employable in the tech industry [4]
- National Education Policy 2020 targets 50% GER in higher education by 2035 [5]
- Grade 8: only 55% of students can divide a two-digit number [6]
- World Bank HCI for India: 0.56 (child born today reaches 56% of potential) [7]
- Learning-adjusted years of school: 4.7 vs. 12.3 expected years [7]
- 62% gap between years in school and years actually learning [7]
- 18% of primary school positions vacant (NSO 2022) [18]

### Education Spending
- Education spending: ~3% of GDP (decades of stagnation) [22]
- NEP 2020 targets 5% by 2035, but no state has moved meaningfully toward target [22]
- Average private school in rural India: ₹25,000 per student/year [23]
- Average developed country school: $50,000 per student/year [23]

### AI Ecosystem
- India AI Summit 2024: 2,300 delegates, 360 speakers, 240 AI startups [8]
- India AI Mission: ₹8,000 crore allocation over four years [9]
- India AI Report 2024: AI startups raised ~₹7,000 crore in funding [14]
- India's AI startup funding is <1% of Chinese AI startup funding [15]
- IIT Madras Kharagwadi: 250+ startups incubated [16]
- IITs collectively produce ~10,000 engineering graduates/year across 23 institutions [11]
- Tsinghua University (China) alone produces 10,000+ engineering graduates/year [13]

### Global Comparisons
- World Bank HCI: South Korea 0.91, China 0.77, Bangladesh 0.57, India 0.56 [7]
- US produces ~50,000 CS PhDs annually vs. India's <1,000 [20][21]
- China's "New Generation AI Development Plan" (2017) targets 10 million AI talent by 2030 [19]

### Demographics
- India's working-age population projected to grow by 200 million by 2040 [2]
- India projected to become world's third-largest economy by 2040 [2]
- IMF AI Preparedness Index: India ranks in lower-middle tier among large economies [24]
